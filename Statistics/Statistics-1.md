Statistics
Types of Statistics
Descriptive Statistics:
Summarizes and describes data.
Examples: Average salary, highest temperature, KPI dashboards.
Inferential Statistics:
Uses sample data to draw conclusions about a population.
Examples: Election prediction, A/B testing, confidence intervals.

 Types of Data
Categorical Data (Qualitative):
Labels or names. Cannot perform mathematical operations.
Examples: Gender, City, Department.
Numerical Data (Quantitative):
1.Discrete Data:
Countable, whole numbers.
Examples: Number of students, number of orders.
2.Continuous Data:
Measured values with decimals.
Examples: Temperature, height, salary.
Levels of Measurement
Nominal:
Categories without ranking.
Examples: Blood group, colors.
Ordinal:
Categories with ranking.
Examples: Customer ratings, Low/Medium/High.
Interval:
Example: Temperature in Celsius.
Ratio:
Examples: Age, salary, sales.

Measures of Central Tendency
Mean:
Average of all values.
Formula: Mean = Sum of values / Number of values
Median:
Middle value after sorting.
Odd: Middle number
Even: Average of two middle numbers
Mode:
Most frequently occurring value.
Measures of Dispersion
Range:
Range = Maximum – Minimum
Variance:
Shows how far data is from the mean.
Standard Deviation:
Square root of variance.
Low SD - data close to mean
High SD -data widely spread.


Distribution Shape
Symmetrical Distribution:
Mean = Median = Mode.
Example: Normal distribution.
Skewed Distribution:
Right Skewed:
Tail on right side. Mean > Median.
Example: Salary data.
Left Skewed:
Tail on left side. Mean < Median.
Example: Retirement age data.
